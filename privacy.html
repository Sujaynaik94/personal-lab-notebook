Privacy Policy – Personal Lab Notebook

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Personal Lab Notebook is a free, open-source web app that runs entirely in your browser. The developer has no server and does not collect, receive, store or sell your data.

Where your data lives

Your notes, protocols, images and settings are stored in your own browser's storage on your own device. Nothing is sent to the developer.

Optional Google Drive sync

If you choose "Sync with Google Drive", the app asks for one permission: access to its own private app folder in your Google Drive (drive.appdata). It cannot see, read or change any other file in your Drive. A single backup file of your notebook is saved there, so you can use it on your other devices. Data goes directly between your browser and Google. The developer never sees it. You can stop syncing at any time and remove the app's access in your Google Account settings (Security → Third-party access); you can then delete the app folder data from there too.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Analytics and cookies

The app uses no analytics, advertising or tracking. It uses browser storage only to keep your own notes and settings.

Contact
